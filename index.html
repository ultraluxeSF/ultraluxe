What I do

Most clients come for a brand and stay for the graphics that keep it alive every week.

Branding and identity

Logos, brand books and complete identity systems: colour, type, patterns, stationery, packaging and merch.

Graphic design

Posters, print, presentations, banners and packaging that clearly belong to one brand.

  • Social media design

    Post and story systems, carousels, covers and YouTube thumbnails built to be produced every week.

  • 3D and motion

    Product renders and logo animation made in Blender.

  • Photo processing

    Retouching, colour correction, upscaling and composites.

  • UI/UX design

    Interfaces for websites and apps, when the brand needs one.

How it works

  1. Brief

    We talk through your business, your audience and what the design has to achieve.

  2. Concepts

    I present directions shown on real applications, so you can judge them in context.

  3. Refinement

    We polish the chosen direction together until every detail works.

  4. Handover

    You get all source files and guidelines, ready for print, web and social.

Hello, I'm Iskandar. I'm a graphic designer who builds brands: logos, identity systems and the everyday graphics that keep a brand alive.

I've worked with clients in Uzbekistan, Russia, Austria, Germany, Switzerland, the UK, Canada, Thailand, Cyprus, Turkey and Chile, from a yarn store in Berlin to an investment company in the UK. Since 2023 I've also designed every Instagram graphic for Inter Nation English School.
